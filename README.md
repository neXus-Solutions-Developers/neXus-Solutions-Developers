## 🔄 How We Work

Our development process is structured around clear requirements, practical execution, and iterative improvement.

**01 — Discover**

Understand the business, target audience, objectives, and project requirements.

**02 — Plan & Design**

Define the scope, structure, user journeys, and visual direction.

**03 — Develop**

Build the solution using technologies suited to the project's requirements.

**04 — Test & Refine**

Check functionality, responsiveness, usability, and common issues.

**05 — Deliver & Improve**

Prepare the final product for delivery and identify opportunities for future improvements.

## 💡 What Guides Us

- **User-focused design:** Interfaces should be intuitive, not unnecessarily complicated.
- **Purposeful development:** Every feature should solve a real problem.
- **Responsive experiences:** Products should work across relevant screen sizes.
- **Maintainable code:** Clear structure matters beyond the initial release.
- **Transparent collaboration:** Scope, progress, and expectations should be communicated clearly.
- **Continuous improvement:** Better products come from testing, feedback, and iteration.
